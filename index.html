FAQ

Questions you might have

Is my data secure?

Role-based access control (owner, admin, analyst, observer) is live today, and database credentials are encrypted at rest and never exposed in chat or logs. Single sign-on (SAML/OIDC) and two-factor authentication are in development and not yet available.

Which data sources do you support?

We currently support PostgreSQL, MySQL, and SQL Server, with more data sources being added during the beta.

How long does onboarding take?

Most teams connect a database and ask their first question the same day — there's no lengthy implementation project.

How does pricing work?

Free, Pro, and Business plans are self-serve — see Pricing above. These are beta prices and may change before general availability. Enterprise plans are custom — contact sales for details.
